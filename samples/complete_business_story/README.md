# Complete synthetic business story

These 22 workbooks form one internally consistent demonstration company: **Banksia Office Supplies Pty Ltd — Synthetic Demo**.

- `setup/` contains the permanent business baseline: statements, chart of accounts, requirements, assets, aged balances, contracts, forecast, people plan, use cases, tax history, market context, customer and supplier masters, inventory and the FY2027 operating budget.
- `recurring/` contains the first operating cycle: open supplier invoices, open sales invoices, six months of banking and June payroll.

Every workbook is labelled `SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA`. Names, ABNs, entities and market evidence are fictional and must not be represented as actual company disclosures.

Control totals:

- Cash equals the bank closing balance: **$186,400**
- Aged debtors equal accounts receivable: **$124,850**
- Aged creditors equal accounts payable: **$97,600**
- Inventory detail equals the balance-sheet inventory control: **$168,300**
- June payroll gross pay: **$41,600**

The packaged `business.db` already contains these sources. The files remain here so the story can be inspected or reloaded after a reset.
