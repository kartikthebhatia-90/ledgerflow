LedgerFlow folder intake

Paste permanent/setup files into the displayed permanent path.
Paste invoices, bank statements, sales invoices and payroll into the displayed recurring path.

Optional: create a document-type subfolder such as:
  file_drop/permanent/cash_flow_statement
  file_drop/permanent/business_requirements
  file_drop/recurring/bank_statements
  file_drop/recurring/payroll

Supported extensions: CSV, XLSX, XLSM and PDF. Files are moved to file_drop/archive after they are queued.
